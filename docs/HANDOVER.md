# Developer handover

## Decisions already made

- The Seed Hub is the long-lived container; projects such as BSF sit inside it.
- The frontend must remain usable before a CMS is selected.
- Unconfirmed institutional and project details are labelled rather than invented.
- A generic discussion forum is deferred until moderation ownership exists.
- The design is mobile-first, low-bandwidth conscious and accessibility-oriented.

## Next technical slice

Implement the CMS schema in a staging environment, create the API-backed content adapter, add preview-mode handling for editors and connect an approved media store. Do not add public self-registration until its purpose, moderation rules, privacy notice and account lifecycle are documented.

## Partner inputs still needed

- Official project title, countries, timeline and donor acknowledgement
- Final institutional owner and coordinating contact
- Approved partner list, logos and biographies
- Current activities, outputs and impact evidence
- Image and video files with permission metadata
- Publication, moderation and response responsibilities
- Required languages and translation workflow
